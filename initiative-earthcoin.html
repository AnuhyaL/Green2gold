EarthCoin & Capstone

The genesis EARTHCOIN NFT, released in support of Earth Day 2022.

EARTHCOIN & CAPSTONE

YoshiDrops.com is proud to partner with Green2Gold on the genesis EARTHCOIN NFT, being released in support of EarthDay 2022. The EARTHCOIN will be released on the WAX blockchain, one of the greenest platforms available to support positive solutions and energize the global green economy. The EARTHCOIN will be exclusively available for purchase for $14.95 starting on April 15 at www.YoshiDrops.com.

As an exciting addition to the genesis EARTHCOIN drop, we're also collecting submissions from creators around the globe to have their music featured on the next release of the EARTHCOIN, happening sometime mid-summer. Enter your music in the EARTHCOIN Music Contest here.
