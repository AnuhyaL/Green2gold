Bringing G2G to your community

How to bring a Green2Gold Incubator To Your Community For Sustainable Inclusive Economic Development

Green2Gold Impact Business Incubators And Accelerators. Green2Gold, a non-profit 501(c)(3) global incubator creator with 54 years of experience in nurturing business development at locations around the world. Our Green Entrepreneurial Innovation Hub is dedicated to advancing Mixed Technology and Mixed Use entities. These include but not limited to: Aerospace, Agriculture, Biotechnology, Energy, Green Chemistry, Healthtech, Renewable Energy of All Types, The Green Economy, Watertech, and more…not to mention a whole spectrum of commercial, industrial, governmental products and services.

Green2Gold's incubator process leverages our years of experience in incubating and hatching successful impact companies, products, and technologies that represent billions of dollars of commerce and thousands of patents. Additionally, we are unique on this planet in that we incubate impactful nonprofits (over 300 to date) that are socially and environmentally responsible, and a unique synergy and symbiotic relationship often grows between the ventures and the nonprofits. Green2Gold is inclusive in every way imaginable and has been since helping found EarthDay and the First International United Nations Conference on the Human Environment.
