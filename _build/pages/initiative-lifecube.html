Lifecube Emergency Shelter

Started as an idea on a napkin…

Rugged · Reliable · Instant shelter

"Rescue The Future"

The Lifecube is a rugged, reliable instant emergency shelter. It's an example of Green2Gold's incubation journey: an idea sketched on a napkin, developed into a real product for disaster response.

Lifecube is also a partner in Build Back Green, the global summit and fundraiser for regenerative disaster recovery after the Maui fire.
