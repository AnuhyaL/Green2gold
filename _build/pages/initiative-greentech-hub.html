The Green Entrepreneurial Innovation Hub

Our Green Entrepreneurial Innovation Hub is dedicated to advancing Mixed Technology and Mixed Use entities. These include but not limited to: Aerospace, Agriculture, Biotechnology, Energy, Green Chemistry, Healthtech, Renewable Energy of All Types, The Green Economy, Watertech, and more…not to mention a whole spectrum of commercial, industrial, governmental products and services.

Green2Gold's incubator process leverages our years of experience in incubating and hatching successful impact companies, products, and technologies that represent billions of dollars of commerce and thousands of patents. Members also have access to prototype development and manufacturing sourcing through the Green2Gold pathways.
