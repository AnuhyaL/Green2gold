Connecting innovators with funding, experts & opportunities

Funding Fridays is Green2Gold Incubator's weekly global funding series. Each week it connects entrepreneurs, inventors, ventures, nonprofits and sustainability leaders with funding opportunities, financial experts and valuable resources.

Explore funding opportunities

  • Equity investment & venture funding
  • Debt financing & alternative capital
  • Grants & non-dilutive funding
  • Unconventional funding pathways
  • Expert insights & funding resources

Calling all presenters & funding experts

Are you an investor, financial expert, funding organization or industry leader? Share your knowledge through a 30-minute live virtual interview or presentation, with time for audience Q&A. Sessions are recorded, and event information is shared with Green2Gold's network of 100,000 members.

Sponsorship opportunities

We welcome organizations offering products and services that support nonprofits, entrepreneurs, sustainable ventures and the inclusive green economy.
